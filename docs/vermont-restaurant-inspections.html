Checking a Vermont restaurant inspection

The Vermont Department of Health's Food and Lodging Program licenses and inspects the restaurants that serve the public, and its own public health inspectors do the inspecting. Its reports are published in one place, the Department's inspection search, and not as open data.

Search the Department of Health's inspectionsThe Food and Lodging Program

What the search shows

The Department's portal covers inspections from May 2016 to the present; for older ones it asks you to email FoodLodging@vermont.gov. A report shows the findings of the last inspection, the inspector's comments and the inspection score. The Department calls an inspection a snapshot of one day and time: violations are often fixed before the inspector leaves, and critical items, the kind that can make someone sick, must be fixed right away.

What Green Mountain Eats does show

No inspection results: the Vermont Department of Health publishes none as data, and Green Mountain Eats computes no grade of its own. Every place in the app and on the web search links to the Department's search. The rest is hand-checked research and James Beard honors.
