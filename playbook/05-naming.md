# Green Mountain Eats: name, icon and palette (provisional, 2026-11-05; Nick decides)

## Name
- **Green Mountain Eats** (App Store name "Green Mountain Eats: Vermont Restaurants", Home Screen "Green Mountain Eats"). One of the
  prompt's three directions. A web search (2026-11-05) found no business, app or publication by that name; the prompt notes the words
  are crowded (Keurig Green Mountain, a Green Mountain Grills app), and USPTO isn't checked yet. Ski Vermont runs a blog series called
  "Vermont Mountain Eats" (a resort-dining column, not an app).
- Avoided: **Vermont Eats** (the Eat Vermont app, App Store id 6736768578, owns that search; vermonteats.com redirects to it).
- Alternatives for Nick: **Vermont Plates** (dishes plus the green license plates; no business or app by that name found 2026-11-05),
  **802 Eats** (the only area code; 802eats.com is registered and parked, and the state's liquor stores are "802 Spirits").
- Not used: the motto "Freedom and Unity", "Vermont Strong", the state seal or arms, the maple leaf (it reads as Canada), the
  Catamount or any school or team mark.

## Icon (state-prompts/ICON-STYLE.md; all three pass state-prompts/icon-style/check.swift)
- **A, chosen provisionally: a maple creemee**, a three-tier maple soft-serve swirl on a waffle cone, on spruce. Reads at 60 px.
- B: a white maple syrup jug with a ring handle and an amber label band. The clearest at 60 px; the best alternate.
- C: a slice of apple pie with a slice of cheddar. Reads as a box at 60 px.
Drawn by `scripts/icon-concepts.swift` (playbook/brand/concept-{a,b,c}-1024.png); `scripts/make-brand.swift` makes the app icon, the
site icons and docs/og.png from A.

## Palette (the prompt's landscape palette; no major-league team plays in Vermont)
spruce #1E4D2B (9.8:1 on white) for titles, tint and the icon background; maple amber #A0521D (white on it 5.7:1) for the hand-checked
guide cards; barn red #9E2A2B (7.5:1) for honors chips and focus rings; syrup gold #D9A441 only as a fill under ink #1A1A1A (7.7:1);
cream #FAF7F0 pages. Header band: the Green Mountains' ridgeline rising to Camel's Hump, over a maple-amber baseline.
